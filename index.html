About A1 Computers

Serving customers since 1998

A1 Computers was established in 1998 and has been serving customers with a wide range of technology products and services.

We deal in laptops, desktop computers, printers, CCTV cameras, mobile accessories and computer spare parts. We are also pioneers in Home Cinema Installation, helping customers create better entertainment experiences at home.

We serve both B2B and B2C customers with products and services across IT, printing, security, sound systems and mobility solutions.

We also deal in refurbished and used laptops, computers, printers and mobile phones.

Our in-house service facility provides repair and service for laptops, printers, computers, mobile phones and other electronic items.
